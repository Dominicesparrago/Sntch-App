Browser simulation of the SNTCH hardware face, menu and QR screens on a 160 x 128 landscape TFT. It is not connected to an ESP32 controller. The QR codes are real and scannable but carry demo text, not a live pairing token. The mascot is a 54 x 52 pixel sprite built from the SNTCH logo and T-pose sheet; faces follow the pixel-art expression sheet.
